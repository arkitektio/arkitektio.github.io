## What Vs is not

The "Vs" deep dives and the "Why Not…?" articles are not a series that explains why Arkitekt is better than other tools, or why a feature we do not support is a bad idea. It is not meant to be a series of articles that explain why other tools are bad. It just stands more as collection of thoughts and ideas that explain why Arkitekt is the way it is.

You disagree with a description in one of these articles? You think we misrepresent a tool, or that Arkitekt should support a feature it does not? Please, please please open a discussion on the [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/orgs/arkitektio/discussions) page. We would love to hear your thoughts and ideas. Really!
