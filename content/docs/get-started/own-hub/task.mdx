You have a hub, Orkestrator and an app. Now you put them to work and see what Arkitekt records
along the way.

## Do it

First you need an image. Basiks can make one:

1. Open **Actions** in the sidebar and search for **Generate Test Image**.
2. Press its play button, choose `human_mitosis` (a microscopy image of nuclei) and press **Do**.
3. The new image pops up in the bottom right corner when it is ready. Click it to open it in the
   viewer.

Now apply an action to it:

4. Right-click the image and choose **Otsu Thresholding** from the actions offered for it.
5. Leave the options as they are and press **Do**.
6. The result, a mask of the nuclei, pops up when it is done. Click it to view it.

## What a task is

Each time you pressed **Do**, you started a **task**: one run of an action, with your inputs. This
is the path it took:

1. **You assigned it to the action, not to a machine.** Orkestrator sent "Otsu Thresholding, on
   this image" to the hub. The hub checked that you may run it and that the inputs fit.
2. **The hub routed it.** It looked for a running app that can do Otsu Thresholding, found Basiks,
   and passed the task on.
3. **The app did the work.** Basiks fetched the image from the hub, computed the mask and stored it
   back on the hub, reporting progress as it went.
4. **The result came back to you.** The hub told Orkestrator the task was done, and the mask popped
   up.

Orkestrator never needed to know where Basiks runs, and Basiks never needed to know who asked. The
hub sits between them. That is also how the right-click menu offered the right actions: the hub
knows which actions take an image as input.

More on tasks, their states and cancelling them in [Task](/docs/concepts/terminology#task).

## Provenance: where every result came from

Arkitekt recorded that the mask was **derived from that image**, by a task running **Otsu
Thresholding**, done by **Basiks**, **started by you**, in **your organization**. Every create,
update and delete on your data is recorded this way, as it happens.

Months from now, anyone in your organization can open the mask and see how it was made, without a
lab notebook. That record is [provenance](/docs/concepts/provenance).

## The whole loop

| Piece | What it did here |
|---|---|
| **Coordinator** (go.arkitekt.live) | Knew who you are, and introduced Orkestrator and Basiks to your hub |
| **Hub** | Stored the image and the mask, and passed the work to Basiks |
| **Organization** | The boundary everything happened in |
| **Apps** | Orkestrator, to ask; Basiks, to do the work |
| **Actions and tasks** | What Basiks can do, and each run of it you started |
| **Provenance** | The record of how the mask came to be |

<Quiz>
  <Question q="What is the difference between an action and a task?">
    An action is what an app can do; a task is one run of it, with your inputs.
  </Question>
  <Question q="When you run an action, do you choose which machine it runs on?">
    No. You assign the task to the action, and the hub routes it to a running app that can do it.
  </Question>
  <Question q="What does provenance record about the mask?">
    That it was derived from the test image, by a task running Otsu Thresholding, done by Basiks,
    started by you, in your organization.
  </Question>
</Quiz>

<Callout type="info" title="Something did not work?">

- **The hub is not in the Assign to hub list.** The hub was not accepted on page 2, or is still
  starting. `konstruktor list` shows the hubs on this machine, and `konstruktor status my-hub` what
  is running.
- **Generate Test Image is not in the Actions list.** The app is not running yet. Open the App
  Store, find Basiks and check that it shows as running.

More in [Troubleshooting](/docs/guides/troubleshooting).

</Callout>
