Your hub is running, but nothing is connected to it yet. On this page you connect **Orkestrator**,
the desktop app you use Arkitekt with.

## Do it

Download Orkestrator and open it:

<OrkestratorGrid />

1. Click **Sign in with go.arkitekt.live**. Your browser opens.
2. Sign in with your account.
3. On the approval page, the hub you just started is already selected under **Assign to hub**.
   Click **Allow access**.

Back in Orkestrator, you are connected to your hub. It is empty, for now.

## What just happened

Orkestrator is an **app**, like every other tool that connects to Arkitekt. Signing in went
through the same three parties every app goes through:

1. **Orkestrator asked the coordinator.** It opened go.arkitekt.live in your browser, so you
   could sign in there. Orkestrator never sees your password.
2. **You decided what it may do.** The approval page showed which hub it wants to use, and on
   whose behalf. **Allow access** gave Orkestrator permission to act for you, on that hub.
3. **The coordinator introduced them.** Orkestrator received a token and the hub's address. From
   then on it talks to the hub directly, and the hub checks the token on every request.

The coordinator is now out of the loop: what you see in Orkestrator comes straight from your hub.
How this works in detail (OAuth2, tokens, scopes) is in
[Identity & Access](/docs/concepts/security/identity).

## Organizations

Everything you do happens inside an **organization**: the group your data belongs to, such as a lab,
a facility or just you. The hub was accepted for your organization, and Orkestrator now acts for you
in it.

The organization is the one boundary in Arkitekt that **always holds**: data never crosses from one
organization to another. Inside it, things are open by default: colleagues you add to it can see and
build on what you make. More in [Organization](/docs/concepts/terminology#organization).
