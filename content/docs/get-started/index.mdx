Everything in Arkitekt runs through a **hub**. So the first question is whether you already have one.

<Cards>
  <Card icon={<Users />} title="I already have a hub" href="/docs/get-started/join">
    Your lab, your imaging facility or a collaborator runs one. You only need the desktop app and an
    invite. No server, no Docker.
  </Card>
  <Card icon={<Server />} title="I want to install a hub" href="/docs/get-started/own-hub">
    Install a hub on your own computer with Docker, in about fifteen minutes. Your data stays with
    you.
  </Card>
</Cards>

Both paths come in two speeds: **super quick** on one page, or **with a coffee**, where each step
explains what you are doing. They end at the same point. Dont you like coffee?

Setting up a hub for a whole lab or facility? That is under
[Advanced deployment](/docs/get-started/deployment/what-you-are-setting-up), and the apps are on
[Downloads](/docs/get-started/downloads).
