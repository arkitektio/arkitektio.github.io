Most people who use Arkitekt never run a <Term id="hub">hub</Term>. Their lab, their imaging facility or a collaborator
runs one, and they connect to it. This page is that path, on one page: no server, no Docker, just
the desktop app. It takes about ten minutes once you have an invite. Want to know what you are
doing as you go? Take the [version with a coffee](/docs/get-started/basics).

## Get into their organization

A hub only lets in members of the [organization](/docs/about/terminology#organization) it belongs
to: a lab, a facility, a group. The way in is an **invite** from someone who is already a member.
Your account says who you are; the invite opens the door.

<Tabs items={["Somebody sent me an invite", "I have no invite yet"]}>
<Tab value="Somebody sent me an invite">

1. **Open the link.** It looks like `https://go.arkitekt.live/invite/…`.
2. **Sign up or log in.** No account yet? Press **Sign Up** on the invite page, with your email
   address, Google, ORCID or your institution's login. You come back to the invite afterwards.
3. **Accept.** You are now a member of their organization, and every hub of it accepts you.

Some invites share only part of someone's work: then you join as a **guest** and see only what they
shared with you, not the whole organization.

Signed in with a different account than the one invited? The invite page tells you so: sign out
and sign back in with the right one.

Your account stores only what a login needs (see the
[privacy policy](/docs/privacy#tier-2-an-account-on-goarkitektlive)). go.arkitekt.live never sees
your data. If the link points somewhere other than go.arkitekt.live, that is the <Term id="coordinator">coordinator</Term> their
hub trusts, and everything else on this page stays the same.

</Tab>
<Tab value="I have no invite yet">

**Who to ask.** Whoever set up Arkitekt where you work. Usually that is your lab's admin (or the
colleague who keeps mentioning Arkitekt), your imaging facility if it runs a hub for the groups using
its microscopes, or a collaborator who wants to share data with you.

**What to ask for.** An invite link to their organization. If they haven't made one before, this is
all they need to know: on [go.arkitekt.live](https://go.arkitekt.live), open the organization, go to
**Invites** and create one. They can copy the link or email it to you from there. Something like:

> Could you invite me to your organization on go.arkitekt.live? Under **Invites** you can create a
> link and send it to me.

While you wait, you can [create your account](https://go.arkitekt.live/account/signup) and install
Orkestrator (step 1 below). When the link arrives, switch to the other tab.

**Nobody to ask?** Then run a hub yourself, in about fifteen minutes with Docker:
[super quick](/docs/get-started/own-hub/super-quick), or
[with explanations](/docs/get-started/basics).

</Tab>
</Tabs>

## Connect and run something

<Steps>
<Step>

### Install Orkestrator and connect

**Orkestrator** is the desktop app you use Arkitekt with. Download it and open it:

<OrkestratorGrid />

1. Click **Sign in with go.arkitekt.live** and sign in, in the browser that opens.
2. On the approval page, pick their hub under **Assign to hub**. If you are only in one
   organization with one hub, it is already selected.
3. Leave **Join the organization's mesh** switched on and click **Allow access**.

You never typed an address. Your account knows the hubs of your organization, and told Orkestrator
where to find the one you picked. If the hub is on another network, say you are at home and it is in
the lab, the [mesh](/docs/run/security/mesh) connects the two, which is what that switch is for.

</Step>
<Step>

### Run something

What you can run depends on which <Term id="app">apps</Term> that hub has installed. Open **Actions** in
the sidebar to see them: each <Term id="action">action</Term> is something one of those apps can do.

If the hub runs **Basiks**, the standard library, you can do the same first run as everyone else:

1. Find **Generate Test Image**, press its play button, choose `human_mitosis` and press **Do**.
2. Click the image that pops up in the bottom right corner to open it.
3. Right-click the image, choose **Otsu Thresholding** and press **Do**.
4. Click the mask that pops up to view it.

No Basiks? Ask whoever runs the hub to install it, or go straight to your own images with
[Upload data](/docs/use/first-steps/upload-data).

</Step>
</Steps>

## Where your data goes on this path

This is the one thing to be clear about when you use someone else's hub:

- **Your data lives on their hub**, on their hardware. Whoever administers it is responsible for it,
  and can reach it. Ask them about backups before you put your only copy of anything there.
- **Your organization can see it.** Inside an organization, data is
  [open by default](/docs/about/terminology#open-by-default-within-your-organization): colleagues in
  the same organization can see and build on what you make, unless the admins have restricted it.
- **We still don't see it.** go.arkitekt.live only knows your account and which organizations you
  are in. Your images go directly between Orkestrator and the hub.

<Callout type="info" title="Something did not work?">

- **"This invite link isn't valid".** The link is incomplete, or the invite expired or was
  cancelled. Ask for a new one.
- **The Assign to hub list is empty.** You haven't accepted an invite yet, or the organization has
  no hub. Check with whoever invited you.
- **The Actions list is empty.** No apps are running on that hub right now. Ask whoever runs it.

More in [Troubleshooting](/docs/use/troubleshooting).
