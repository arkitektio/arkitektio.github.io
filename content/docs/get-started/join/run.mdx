You are connected. Now you run something, and see what Arkitekt records along the way.

## Do it

Open **Actions** in the sidebar. What you see there depends on which apps their hub runs. If it runs
**Basiks**, the standard library:

1. Find **Generate Test Image**, press its play button, choose `human_mitosis` (a microscopy image
   of nuclei) and press **Do**.
2. The new image pops up in the bottom right corner. Click it to open it.
3. Right-click the image and choose **Otsu Thresholding** from the actions offered for it.
4. Press **Do**, and click the mask that pops up to view it.

No Basiks? Ask whoever runs the hub to install it, or go straight to your own images with
[Upload data](/docs/guides/using/upload-data).

## Apps and actions

The hub does no analysis itself. **Apps** do: programs that connect to the hub, such as Basiks,
napari, Fiji, a Python script or a segmentation model on a GPU server. When an app connects, it tells
the hub what it can do. Each of those things is an **action**, described by its inputs and outputs:
"Otsu Thresholding: Image → Mask".

The **Actions** list is every action of every app connected to the hub right now. On somebody
else's hub, those apps run wherever its admins started them. You never need to know where.

## What a task is

Each time you pressed **Do**, you started a **task**: one run of an action, with your inputs.

1. **You assigned it to the action, not to a machine.** The hub checked that you may run it and
   that the inputs fit.
2. **The hub routed it** to a running app that can do it.
3. **The app did the work**, fetching the image from the hub and storing the mask back on it.
4. **The result came back** to you.

## Provenance

Arkitekt recorded that the mask was **derived from that image**, by a task running **Otsu
Thresholding**, done by **Basiks**, **started by you**, in **your organization**. On a shared hub
this matters even more: months from now, any colleague can open the mask and see exactly how it was
made, and by whom. That record is [provenance](/docs/concepts/provenance).

## Where your data lives now

- **On their hub**, on their hardware. Whoever administers it is responsible for it, and can reach
  it. Ask them about backups before you put your only copy of anything there.
- **Visible to your organization.** Inside an organization, data is open by default.
- **Not with us.** go.arkitekt.live only knows your account and your organizations.
