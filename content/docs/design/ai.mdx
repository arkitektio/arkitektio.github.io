<Callout type="info" title="TLDR">
We don't hate AI, but we don't trust it either. 
</Callout>

## Looks are deceiving

The current state of AI annoys us, and looking at Arkitekt you might just feel, well you
look like any other AI-generated content yourself, so why do you complain?
The reality is that the look of Arkitekt was designed around established modern
ui frameworks like Shadcn and Tailwind, way before they became the standard of UI
design that every 500 millionth copy of a dashbard now looks like.

Arkitekt is a product that was first conecptualized in 2018 and has been torn down
and rebuilt multiple times since then. Our original build of Arkitekt that this
one inherited almost everything from was built without any AI assistance. This
one was built with AI, but look at the code yourself, and you will find our
stupidness all over..

## What we don't do

We don't *vibe-code*. We don't generate a feature, watch it compile, and merge it
because the demo looked fine. Every line that lands in Arkitekt is read,
understood, and owned by a human who could have written it themselves and can
explain why it is there.

<Callout type="warn" title="No unsupervised code">
AI never merges to Arkitekt on its own. There is always a human in the loop who
reviews, questions, and takes responsibility for the result. If we can't explain
a piece of code, it doesn't ship.
</Callout>

Scientific software is justdiffenrent. People make decisions about real
experiments and real data based on what it does. "The model probably got it
right" is not an acceptable standard for us and a platform that sits between a
microscope and a theory.

## Where we use it

We use AI where it genuinely makes us faster without making us dumber:

- **Drafting.** First passes of components, migrations, and boilerplate that we
  then rewrite, tighten, and review.
- **Refactoring.** Large mechanical changes across the codebase, always checked
  diff by diff.
- **Exploration.** Trying three approaches quickly to find the one worth keeping.

In every case the AI is an accelerator, not an author of record. The judgment,
the architecture, and the responsibility stay with the people building Arkitekt.

## Why this matters for you

If you build on Arkitekt, you are trusting it with data and decisions. You should
know that its polished look is not a shortcut. It is the result of using powerful
tools *under supervision*, the same way we would expect any careful engineer to
use them.

We think that is the honest place to stand on AI. We neither pretend it away, nor
let it run unattended. Use it heavily. Supervise it always.

## A personal note

Arkitekt was conceptualized and largely written before the AI boom. Auto-complete was a lovable addition to
our workflow, and Arkitekt would arguably have taken a year longer without it. Agentic workflows that try to
"improve" every sentence and every function unasked are a different thing, and they leave a bitter taste: the
same one this page is about. Human judgment first, tools second. For the platform-level version of that rule,
where every task must be started by a human, see [Humans first](/docs/design/philosophy/humans-first).
