**Last updated:** July 8, 2026

<Callout type="info" title="The short version">
We don't want your data. None of it. We are not an ad company, we don't
profile you, we don't sell anything, and there is no growth team measuring your
every click. Everything below exists so that the parts of Arkitekt that *do*
touch personal data (mostly: your email, so we can log you in) are handled
honestly and lawfully. If you only read the docs, we know essentially nothing
about you, and we'd like to keep it that way.
</Callout>

This page explains, as plainly as possible, what data we collect, why, and what
happens to it. If anything here is unclear, email us, see contact details at the
bottom.

## Who we are

Arkitekt is operated by Johannes Roos, based in Austria. For GDPR purposes, we
are the **data controller** for the personal data described below.

## Different parts, different rules

Arkitekt is not one single service, and the amount of data involved depends
entirely on *which* part you use. Most people only ever use the first tier, and
for that tier we collect no personal data at all beyond what any web server sees.

Find the row that matches what you're doing:

| What you use | Where it lives | What that means for your data |
|---|---|---|
| **Just reading the docs / website** | Static site on **GitHub Pages**, fronted by **Cloudflare** | No account, no sign-up, no tracking. The only data involved is standard web-server logging (your IP, briefly). Nothing is stored about *you*. |
| **An account on `go.arkitekt.live`** | Our hosted account service | We store your email address so you can log in and receive account emails. Transactional email is sent via **Resend**. That's the extent of it. |
| **Signing in with a social provider** | GitHub / Google / ORCID etc. | We receive a minimal identity token (email + name) from the provider you chose, only to create your login. We don't get access to your provider account. |
| **`demo.arkitekt.live`** | Our public demo instance | ⚠️ Whatever you upload is **stored, and currently stored unencrypted**. This is a playground, treat it as public. See the warning below. |

Each tier is described in more detail below. If a section doesn't match what you
use, it doesn't apply to you.

## Tier 1: Reading the docs and website

The documentation and marketing site are **static files**. They are hosted on
**GitHub Pages** and served through **Cloudflare** for DNS and CDN/security.

- There is **no account, no login, no sign-up** involved in reading the docs.
- We run **no analytics, no tracking cookies, no advertising cookies**. There is
  no consent banner because there is genuinely nothing to consent to.
- The only data that exists is the ordinary technical logging that GitHub Pages
  and Cloudflare perform for *any* website: your IP address, timestamp,
  requested URL, and user agent, used for delivery, caching, and abuse
  protection. We do not receive, store, or analyse this ourselves beyond what
  those providers expose for operational security.

If reading the docs is all you do, this is the entire story. We do not know who
you are, and we don't want to.

## Tier 2: An account on `go.arkitekt.live`

To use hosted Arkitekt features you may create an account. Here we deliberately
ask for **the bare minimum**.

### What we collect, and why

| Data | Why we collect it | Legal basis |
|---|---|---|
| Email address | To create your account and send account-related emails (confirmation, password reset, important notices) | Necessary to perform our contract with you (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)) |
| Name (if you provide one) | To personalise communication, entirely optional | Your consent / contract |

We do **not** ask for anything beyond what's needed to create and secure your
account. No phone number, no address, no company, no "how did you hear about us",
no profiling fields. If a field isn't needed to log you in or keep your account
safe, we don't have it.

### Email delivery (Resend)

Account emails (signup confirmation, password resets) are sent through
[**Resend**](https://resend.com/legal/privacy-policy). To send you an email,
Resend necessarily sees your email address, your name if you gave one, and the
content of that email. We have email **open-tracking and click-tracking turned
off**, we don't want to know whether you opened an email.

### Cookies

If we set a cookie at all, it is strictly a **session cookie** required to keep
you logged in. Nothing else is stored on your device, and no cross-site tracking
happens.

## Tier 3: Signing in with a social provider

If you choose "sign in with GitHub / Google / ORCID" (or another provider), that
provider hands us a minimal identity token, typically your **email address and
name**, purely so we can create or match your Arkitekt login.

- We only receive the specific fields you approve on the provider's consent
  screen. We do **not** get access to your account, your repositories, your
  contacts, or anything else.
- The provider is an independent controller for their own processing, your
  relationship with GitHub, Google, ORCID, etc. is governed by *their* privacy
  policy, not this one.
- Once you're logged in, the data we hold is the same minimal set as Tier 2:
  email, and name if provided.

## Tier 4: `demo.arkitekt.live`

<Callout type="warn" title="Please don't put anything real here">
`demo.arkitekt.live` is a public playground so you can try Arkitekt without
installing anything. Whatever you upload to it is **stored on our server, and
currently stored unencrypted**. Treat everything on the demo as effectively
public and disposable. **Do not upload sensitive, personal, confidential, or
unpublished research data to the demo.** We may reset or wipe it at any time.
</Callout>

The demo exists purely for convenience and evaluation. If you want to work with
real data, run your own Arkitekt instance, where the data stays on
infrastructure *you* control and we never see it.

## Who else touches your data (our processors)

We use a small number of external services to run Arkitekt. Each only receives
the minimum data needed to do its job, and each operates under a Data Processing
Agreement (DPA) where applicable.

| Service | What it's used for | What data it sees | Where it's based |
|---|---|---|---|
| **GitHub Pages** | Hosting the static docs/website | IP address (standard web-server logging) | United States |
| **Cloudflare** | DNS, CDN and security for the site | IP address of site visitors, as part of normal web traffic | Global network, EU-based option available depending on config |
| **Resend** | Sending transactional account emails | Your email address, name (if given), and the content of the email we send you | United States |
| **Social login providers** (GitHub, Google, ORCID, …) | Authenticating you, only if you choose social login | The identity fields you approve, typically email + name | Varies by provider |

**We do not sell, rent, or share your data with advertisers.** We do not use
these services' marketing or tracking features, for example, email open/click
tracking in Resend is turned **off**.

## Your rights

Under GDPR (and similar laws elsewhere), you have the right to:

- **Access** — ask what data we hold about you
- **Rectification** — ask us to correct inaccurate data
- **Erasure** — ask us to delete your data ("right to be forgotten")
- **Portability** — get a copy of your data in a machine-readable format
- **Object / restrict processing** — ask us to stop or limit how we use your data
- **Withdraw consent** — where processing is based on consent, at any time

To exercise any of these, email us (below). We'll respond within 30 days, as
required by GDPR. For most requests on a project this size, we can typically
handle it much faster.
