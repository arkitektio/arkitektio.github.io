Most of the work in Arkitekt happens at a desk: you build workflows, look at volumes and annotate data in
[Orkestrator](/docs/use/guides/apps/orkestrator). But a lot of lab life happens away from the desk, next to a
microscope, at the bench or on the way home while an overnight analysis is still running. For those moments
there is **[Pokket](/docs/use/guides/apps/pokket)**, the mobile companion app.

![Pokket App](/docs/apps/standalones/pokket.png)

## A companion, not a replacement

Pokket does not try to be Orkestrator on a small screen. There is no workflow editor and no image viewer.
It talks to the same server, with the same account, and is built around the few things that are actually
useful when all you have is a phone:

- **Keeping an eye on tasks.** Pokket lists your latest tasks from the `rekuest` service, so you can check
  whether that long-running segmentation finished, or failed, without walking back to your computer.
- **Bringing hardware online.** Pokket is how small devices get into Arkitekt. An ESP32 running the
  [Arkitekt firmware](/docs/build/tutorials/embedded-first-device) advertises itself over Bluetooth; Pokket sends it your Wi-Fi
  credentials (eduroam included) and an Arkitekt connection token over the **Improv Wi-Fi** protocol. The device
  then connects, registers its actions, and shows up as an app in Orkestrator. Saved Wi-Fi profiles let you set up
  a whole shelf of devices without retyping credentials.
- **Reaching your server from anywhere.** If your organisation runs a [mesh](/docs/deploy/concepts/security/mesh), Pokket
  can join it after you approve it, the same way Orkestrator does. It runs its own node inside the app, so it
  needs no VPN permission and no separate Tailscale app, and services that only live on the mesh become reachable
  from your phone.

Pokket signs in through `lok`, like every other Arkitekt app, so what you can see and do on your phone is exactly
what your account is allowed to see and do on the server.

## Getting Pokket

Pokket is available for **Android** as an APK and for **iPhone / iPad** through TestFlight:

- **Android:** open [`pokket.apk`](https://github.com/arkitektio/pokket/releases/latest/download/pokket.apk) on
  your phone and install it. This link always points to the latest release.
- **iOS:** ask a maintainer for a TestFlight invite.

Once installed, Pokket keeps itself up to date: most updates arrive over the air when you open the app. See the
[Pokket page](/docs/use/guides/apps/pokket#install) for details.

<Callout type="info" title="Device provisioning needs Bluetooth">

Setting up ESP32 devices uses Bluetooth Low Energy, so your phone needs BLE and Pokket needs permission to use it.
The [embedded guide](/docs/build/tutorials/embedded-first-device) walks through flashing a device and provisioning it with Pokket.

</Callout>

<Callout type="warn" title="Live streams over the mesh">

When Pokket reaches your server through the mesh, regular requests and live updates are carried, but WebRTC
media (LiveKit) is not. Live video streams will not play on the phone over the mesh.

</Callout>
