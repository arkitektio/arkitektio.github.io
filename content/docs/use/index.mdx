Both paths, as written, start with an **account on [go.arkitekt.live](https://go.arkitekt.live)**,
our hosted coordination server.

<Callout type="warn" title="Wait, I need an account?">

  No. You can run Arkitekt without ever talking to us: point Konstruktor at your institute's own
  coordination server, or [self-host everything](/docs/deploy/concepts/topology#three-ways-to-meet),
  coordinator included, and stay fully offline. The account just makes things easier: go.arkitekt.live
  is a [coordinator](/docs/deploy/concepts/security/identity) we already run, so there is nothing extra to
  operate and the [mesh](/docs/deploy/concepts/security/mesh) works across networks out of the box. It stores
  only what a login needs (see the [privacy policy](/docs/privacy#tier-2-an-account-on-goarkitektlive)),
  and your data and compute never go through it. If you would rather not, read
  [Where Arkitekt runs](/docs/deploy/concepts/topology) for the trade-offs and pick the
  **Another server** option in Konstruktor.

</Callout>
