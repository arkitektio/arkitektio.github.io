## Welcome

Glad you are here, and welcome to our Arkitekt docs! Here you will find everything you need to know about Arkitekt, 
from the [big picture](/docs/concepts/philosophy) to the nitty-gritty [details](/docs/concepts)


## Arkitekt in a nutshell

Arkitekt is an open-source platform for bioimage analysis. It does not replace your microscope
software, napari or Fiji. It is the "middleman" that connects them:

- A **hub** sits in the middle. It is a server that your lab runs: it stores your images and
  results, and it passes work between your tools.
- **Apps** connect to the hub: Orkestrator (the desktop interface), napari, Fiji, a microscope, or a
  script you wrote. Apps never talk to each other directly, only through the hub.
- **Workflows** chain what those apps can do, across machines and in real time.

[Design](/docs/concepts) has the longer version.

## Using Arkitekt

Somebody in your lab or facility already runs Arkitekt, and you want to work with it. You will learn to:

- **Get connected.** Install Orkestrator and [join their server](/docs/guides/joining).
- **Find your way around.** Upload data, run a tool and build your first workflow in
  [First Steps](/docs/tutorials/absolute-basics).
- **Go further.** Deep learning, local workflows and live analysis in
  [A bit more advanced](/docs/tutorials/advanced).
- **Pick your apps.** What Orkestrator, napari, Fiji and the others do, in [Apps](/docs/guides/apps).
- **Get unstuck.** Common problems and where to ask, in [Troubleshooting](/docs/guides/troubleshooting).
- **Understand the ideas.** [Basics](/docs/concepts/basics) and the
  [Terminology](/docs/concepts/terminology) the rest of the docs use.

As a platform **by humans, for humans**, we suggest you start with one of our guided tutorials, made
by our team and the tutorials community. They are designed to get you up and running quickly, and to give you a feel for what Arkitekt can do.

## Running Arkitekt

Nobody runs Arkitekt for you yet, so you set up a hub of your own. You will learn to:

- **Know what you are setting up.** What a hub is made of, and what Konstruktor does for you after
  the first start, in [What you are setting up](/docs/tutorials/what-you-are-setting-up).
- **Install a hub.** With the Konstruktor desktop app in [Install your first hub](/docs/tutorials/first-hub),
  or [from the command line](/docs/guides/install-cli).
- **Keep it running.** Plugin engines, updates, the mesh and the GPU in [Running a hub](/docs/guides/hub).
- **Plan a deployment.** [Where Arkitekt runs](/docs/concepts/topology), what to
  [consider before you deploy](/docs/concepts/planning) and
  [other installation strategies](/docs/guides/other-installation-strategies).
- **Keep it safe.** Identity, data and the mesh in [Security & Privacy](/docs/concepts/security).
- **Look things up.** [Requirements](/docs/reference/requirements) and
  [Konstruktor](/docs/reference/konstruktor).
- **Get unstuck.** [Help! My server doesn't start up](/docs/guides/troubleshooting/deployment).

## Building with Arkitekt

You write code and want it to become part of the platform. You will learn to:

- **Write your first app.** In [Python](/docs/tutorials/python-first-app),
  [Rust](/docs/tutorials/rust-first-app) or on an
  [embedded device](/docs/tutorials/embedded-first-device).
- **Know the model.** The [core concepts](/docs/concepts/python#core-concepts) of the Python
  library, and [why you would build on Arkitekt](/docs/concepts/why-build) at all.
- **Build real apps.** [Types and data](/docs/guides/python/types),
  [state and lifecycle](/docs/guides/python/state-lifecycle),
  [workflows](/docs/guides/python/workflows) and [packaging](/docs/guides/python/packaging).
- **Understand the hub.** Its [services](/docs/concepts/services),
  [service discovery](/docs/concepts/service-discovery) and [GraphQL](/docs/concepts/graphql).
- **Look things up.** The [manifest](/docs/reference/manifest), the
  [Fakts protocol](/docs/reference/fakts-protocol) and
  [plugin configuration](/docs/reference/plugin-config).
- **Contribute.** How to [get involved](/docs/guides/contribute).
