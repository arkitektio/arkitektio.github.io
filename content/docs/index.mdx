Arkitekt is an open-source platform for bioimage analysis. It doesn't replace your microscope
software, napari or Fiji: it connects them. A **hub** in the middle stores your images and results,
**apps** connect to it and do the work, and **workflows** chain what they can do, across machines and
in real time.
