## Help! [#help]

Arkitekt has many parts, and most errors come from a part stuck in a state nobody anticipated.
Two things fix most of them.

**Restart the smallest part that could cause the problem.**

- A problem with one app: restart the app. Most often an app has lost its connection to the Rekuest
  service, and a restart makes it reconnect.
- A problem with a plugin: restart the plugin.
- A workflow that is scheduled wrongly: restart its scheduler.
- A problem with Orkestrator: reload or restart it.
- Only then the hub. Restarting it also restarts every connected app.

Restarting does not lose data. The hub keeps its data and state in its database and file storage,
not in the running services.

![Reload and restart](/docs/troubleshooting/turn.jpeg)

**Update.** The newest version may already fix your problem. The hub and Orkestrator update
separately:

- The hub: [Update a hub](/docs/get-started/deployment/running#update-a-hub).
- Orkestrator: the [latest release](https://github.com/arkitektio/orkestrator/releases/latest).
- Apps: check whether a newer version exists.
