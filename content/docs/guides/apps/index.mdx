Apps come in two kinds:

- **Standalone apps** are installed on any computer that can reach your hub, and bring their own
  interface. Orkestrator, napari and Fiji are standalone apps.
- **Plugin apps** are containers that your hub's plugin engine runs for you. You install them from
  the App Store in Orkestrator, as in [Install an app](/docs/get-started/own-hub/app).

To write your own, start with [Your first app](/docs/develop/first-python-app).

## Standalone apps

| App | What it does |
|---|---|
| [Orkestrator](/docs/guides/apps/orkestrator) | The desktop app you use Arkitekt with: data, actions, workflows and runs. |
| [Mikro-Napari](/docs/guides/apps/mikro-napari) | Connects napari, to show images and ROIs from your hub. |
| [ImageJ plugin](/docs/guides/apps/imagej-plugin) | Connects Fiji, to upload and show images and run macros as actions. |
| [Gucker](/docs/guides/apps/gucker) | Watches a folder and streams new files into a workflow. |
| [Pokket](/docs/guides/apps/pokket) | The mobile companion: tasks, the mesh and device setup on your phone. |

## Plugin apps

The App Store's default catalog holds the plugin apps the Arkitekt team publishes: Basiks, the file
converters, HoverFast and others. Kare and Segmentor below come from the earlier platform and are
not in the default catalog; their repositories are under
[arkitektio-apps](https://github.com/arkitektio-apps).

### Kare

CARE (content-aware restoration of fluorescence microscopy images) as a plugin app. It trains a
CARE model on your data and restores images with it. See
[Train a deep learning model](/docs/legacy/training).

### Segmentor

StarDist as a plugin app, with a pretrained model for nuclei. See
[Bridging deep learning](/docs/guides/using/deep-learning).
